Fubar's SSTV Decoder includes the MMSSTV decoding engine by JE3HHT Makoto Mori.
Decoded images are shown inside Fubars. No separate MMSSTV application is installed.

The next page lets you skip components already installed. Existing components start
unchecked; select them again if you want an update or reinstall.

The installer can also open the separate VB-CABLE driver setup. VB-CABLE is made by
VB-Audio: https://www.vb-cable.com/ . VB-CABLE is donationware; all contributions
are welcome. Its original driver package is also kept in the installed VB-CABLE folder.

The driver setup requests administrator permission. Restart Windows after installing
the driver. Fubars selects "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)" inside the software.
Launch Fubars after the reboot. It sends SDR receiver audio to CABLE Input.

Existing settings, received images, and downloaded satellite data are preserved.
